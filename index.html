What Is Liquid Coffee Concentrate?

Liquid coffee concentrate is brewed coffee reduced to its purest, most potent form. Think of it as the essence of espresso — without the machine.

Just 10ml of No Sleep Tonight makes a full 100ml cup. No grinding, no brewing, no waiting. It's new to India, but it's been the quiet secret of specialty cafés worldwide for years.

1

Pour 10ml of No Sleep Tonight

Use the measuring spoon included in every order. Go up to 15ml for a stronger, bolder cup.

2

Add Your Liquid

Top up with 90ml of hot water, cold water, cold milk, hot milk — whatever you have.

3

Stir and Done

Give it a quick stir. For iced coffee, pour over ice. Done in under 60 seconds. No machine, no mess.
